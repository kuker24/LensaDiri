# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

LensaDiri serves Indonesian adults exploring patterns in how they think, feel, relate, and act. They often arrive curious but cautious: they want useful language for reflection without being boxed into a diagnosis or absolute personality label. The experience is mobile-first, with desktop support for deeper reading.

## Product Purpose

LensaDiri provides a private, modular self-reflection journey. Users choose one or more lenses, answer structured questions, then receive a transparent report with evidence position, confidence, limitations, and practical reflection prompts. Success means a user can begin, complete, understand, and control their result without confusion or exaggerated claims.

## Positioning

Many lenses, one honest view: structured self-reflection that keeps uncertainty visible and the user in control.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, intimate. The interface should feel premium and confident without becoming clinical, mystical, performative, or exclusive. Indonesian copy stays warm, direct, and non-judgmental.

## Anti-references

- Entertainment quizzes that promise a definitive identity.
- Clinical dashboards that make reflection feel like a medical record.
- Generic SaaS card grids, decorative glass everywhere, gradient text, fake metrics, or invented social proof.
- Cybersecurity copy, travel metaphors, or airport terminology copied literally from the visual reference.
- Neon gaming aesthetics where accent color overwhelms content.

## Design Principles

1. Make privacy and scientific limits visible before persuasion.
2. Let the user move at a calm pace without hiding progress or consequences.
3. Use visual drama for orientation, then let task interfaces disappear into the work.
4. Preserve uncertainty as useful information, never as a defect to conceal.
5. Earn premium quality through typography, spacing, interaction, and restraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive web for keyboard, pointer, and touch use. Preserve semantic landmarks, visible labels, clear focus, 44px minimum interactive targets, screen-reader announcements, contrast-safe text and controls, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not claim WCAG certification without formal external evidence.
